Report Illegal or Abusive Content

In accordance with the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), Kavast provides a mechanism for users and authorities to report content in our Directory that is illegal or violates our Terms of Service.

1. Reporting Mechanism

If you encounter hardware-generated or user-published content in the Kavast Directory (such as cupping session names, nicknames, or notes) that you believe is illegal or harmful, please follow these steps:

  1. Identify the content: Copy the Session URL/ID or take a screenshot.
  2. Email us: Send a report to [INSERT_EMAIL].
  3. Include details: Describe why you believe the content is illegal or abusive.

2. Handling of Reports

Upon receiving a report, Kavast will:

  • Acknowledge receipt of the report.
  • Review the content against our Terms of Service and applicable laws.
  • Take action (if necessary), which may include removing the content or suspending the user's account.
  • Notify the reporter of the decision and action taken.
